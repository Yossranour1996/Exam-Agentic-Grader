	- Q1.5 Inheritance vs. Polymorphism: You defined inheritance but didn’t define polymorphism. Add: polymorphism lets the same method call (through a common interface/superclass) behave differently at runtime depending on the object’s actual type. Ref: https://docs.oracle.com/javase/tutorial/java/IandI/polymorphism.html
	- Q1.6 Encapsulation: Missing. It means bundling data with methods that operate on that data and hiding internal details via access modifiers. Ref: https://docs.oracle.com/javase/tutorial/java/concepts/ (OOP concepts)
	- Q1.7 Abstraction: You wrote “abstract modifiers.” Examiners expect “abstract classes” and “interfaces” as the two mechanisms. Ref: https://docs.oracle.com/javase/tutorial/java/IandI/abstract.html and https://docs.oracle.com/javase/tutorial/java/IandI/createinterface.html
